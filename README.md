# SWANA

Smart Web Analytics & Narrative Assistant By Alyaan Mir and Amaan Azim

## Overview

SWANA is an AI-powered Business Intelligence platform that transforms raw business datasets into interactive dashboards, automated insights, and executive reports.

Users can upload CSV or Excel files and receive:

- Interactive dashboards
- KPI metrics
- AI-generated business insights
- Executive summaries
- Downloadable reports
